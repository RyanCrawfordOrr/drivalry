# App-store name research

- Drivalry records drives, produces driving scores, and supports friendly leagues and social comparison (`C:\Drivalry\README.md` and `C:\Drivalry\store\listing.md`). The user wants a professional, direct store-page name.
- Apple recommends a distinctive, simple name that hints at the app's function, with a maximum of 30 characters. Its subtitle can add more detail and is also limited to 30 characters: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/
- Google Play limits titles to 30 characters and requires relevant, accurate metadata: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en
- An existing UK app uses the separate brand “DriveScore”; avoiding that exact concatenated brand name reduces potential confusion. This is not a trademark clearance: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/drivescore-save-on-insurance/id1570100560
- Proposed cross-platform name: “Drivalry: Driving Scores.” Potential iOS subtitle: “Drive better with friends.” Retain “Good drives. Good company.” as the warmer brand line.
