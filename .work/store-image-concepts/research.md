# Store image concept research

- Product source: `C:\Drivalry\README.md`, `store/listing.md`, `store/README.md`, `app/lib/app/theme.dart`. Drivalry scores smooth driving, supports friends and weekly leagues; visual identity is signal orange `#FF6A1A`, teal `#13C2A3`, dark ink `#111726`.
- Apple: screenshots should reflect the in-use experience; concise, legible text and a clear focal point. Accepted large-display iPhone screenshot size includes 1320 × 2868. PNG without alpha. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/ and https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/
- Google: showcase in-app experiences accurately, minimal taglines, avoid unverified claims and misleading representations. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723?hl=en and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en
